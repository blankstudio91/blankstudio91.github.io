Contact support

Numbers look off, or have an idea? Send us a message. If an estimate seems wrong, tell us your state, pay type (salary or hourly) and workday length. Please don't send your exact salary or other financial details.

We only use what you send to reply to you.

Common questions

How does Live Wage work out my take-home pay?

It starts from the pay and hours you enter, then subtracts estimated federal, state and payroll taxes for the state you choose. The result is an estimate. Your real paycheck can differ because of withholding choices, benefits, retirement contributions and other deductions.

Is my salary sent anywhere?

No. Your pay details, state and work history are saved only on your iPhone. Live Wage has no account and doesn't send your information to us or anyone else.

What happens if I close the app during a shift?

Your workday is saved on your device, so the timer picks up where it left off when you open Live Wage again.

How do I delete my data?

Use Clear stats history in the app to remove your work history, or delete the app to remove everything it has stored.

Privacy policy

Live Wage · Effective October 25, 2026

Live Wage shows how much you earn in real time during a workday, based on pay details you enter. This policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and the choices available to you. In short: everything you enter stays on your device, and we do not collect it.

1. Information you enter

To calculate your earnings, Live Wage asks for pay details such as your salary or hourly rate, pay schedule, usual hours and start time, and the U.S. state (and, where relevant, locality) used to estimate taxes. You can also optionally enter a name, a savings target, and names for your income streams.

This information, along with your workday history, earnings statistics, streaks and achievements, is stored only on your device. It is not uploaded to our servers, and we cannot see it.

2. Information we do not collect

Live Wage does not require an account and does not collect your contact details, precise location, contacts, or device identifiers. The app does not use your device's GPS; the state you choose is entered by you. The app contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking tools, and we do not sell or share personal information.

3. How your information is used

The information you enter is used on your device only to calculate your estimated earnings and take-home pay, run your workday timer, and show your insights, goals, and achievements.

4. Sharing

If you use Share weekly summary, the app creates an image of your summary and opens the iOS share sheet. The image is sent only to the app or person you choose, and that app's privacy policy applies from then on.

5. Backups and diagnostics

Because your data is stored on your device, it may be included in your device backups (such as iCloud or computer backups) according to your own settings; those backups are handled by Apple under its privacy policy. The app may write anonymous diagnostic messages to your device's system log, which do not include your pay details. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iOS settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports.

6. Deleting your data

You can remove your work history at any time with Clear stats history in the app, edit or remove your pay details in the app, or delete the app to remove all data it has stored on your device.

7. Contacting us

If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your name, email address, and message. We use it only to respond to you and to improve the app, and we delete it when it is no longer needed. Please do not send us your salary or other financial details.

8. Children

Live Wage is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through support, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date above will show when it was last updated, and material changes will be communicated when required.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the contact form or email [SUPPORT EMAIL].
