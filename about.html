About

A small studio with a long list of ideas.

Blank Studio is an independent app studio. Every app here is designed, built and supported in-house — which keeps them small, focused and honest.

The name is the method: start with a blank page, find one question worth answering, and build only what answers it. TrackLore and ChessCapture are the first two. More are on the way.
