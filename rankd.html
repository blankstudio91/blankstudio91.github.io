Privacy

Your stats are yours. They stay on your phone.

Everything you enter about yourself is saved only on your iPhone, with Apple's strongest file protection. Rank'd has no account, no ads and no servers. You decide what appears on any card you share.

  • Saved only on this deviceNothing is uploaded or synced to us.
  • You control sharingHide money, wellness or category details from shared cards.
  • Reset anytimeReset all data in Settings, or delete the app.
