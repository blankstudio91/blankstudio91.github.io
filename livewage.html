Your salary is a yearly number. Your time isn't.

  1. 01

    Set your pay

    Enter your salary or hourly rate, your usual hours and the state you work in. Live Wage estimates your take-home pay after taxes as you type.

  2. 02

    Start your workday

    Tap start and watch your earnings tick up by the second and the minute. Pause for breaks, and end the day when you clock out.

  3. 03

    See where it goes

    Weekly summaries, where your pay goes, streaks and a savings target show what your time actually adds up to.

Privacy

Your pay stays between you and your phone.

Your salary, your state and your work history are saved only on your iPhone. Live Wage has no account, no ads and no servers, so your numbers never leave your device.
