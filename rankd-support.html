Common questions

How is my overall score worked out?

Rank'd scores each category you've rated (Fitness, Wellness, Money, Learning, Career and Life) from the stats you enter, then combines them into one overall rating. Your tier, from Bronze to Legend, comes from that score. The more stats you add, the higher your confidence score. You can choose which categories and metrics count in Settings.

Is my information sent anywhere?

No. Your profile, stats, goals, friends and history are saved only on your iPhone. Rank'd has no account and doesn't send your information to us. The only exception is the optional Game Center leaderboard: if you turn on Publish my OVR, your overall score (and nothing else) is sent to Apple's Game Center.

What does Rank'd read from Apple Health?

Only if you allow it: VO₂ max, resting heart rate, steps, sleep, workouts, body fat, weight and biological sex. Rank'd only reads, never writes to Health, and uses this data on your device only to fill in your stats.

How do friends work?

You add friends to your squad yourself, with their permission, by entering their stats. They're saved on your device only. To compare with someone who isn't nearby, share your card or a head-to-head comparison as an image.

Can I hide things on my shared card?

Yes. In Settings you can choose whether shared cards show category scores, money or income, wellness details and other sensitive stats. Your full data stays on your device either way.

How do I delete my data?

Use Reset All Data in Settings to remove your profile, stats, goals, friends and progress history, or delete the app to remove everything it has stored.

Privacy policy

Rank'd · Effective October 25, 2026

Rank'd turns information you enter about yourself into scores and a shareable player card. This policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and the choices available to you. In short: everything you enter stays on your device, and we do not collect it.

1. Information you enter

To build your card, Rank'd lets you enter information about yourself, such as your name, age, height, weight, an optional photo and tagline, and stats in the categories you choose to rate. These can include fitness (such as workouts, push-ups, mile time and steps), wellness (such as sleep, body fat, resting heart rate and vision), money (such as monthly income, savings and debt), learning, career (such as education, certifications, experience and job title) and lifestyle. Every stat is optional. You can also create goals (“quests”), weekly check-ins and card preferences.

This information, along with your scores and progress history, is stored only on your device using iOS file protection. It is not uploaded to our servers, and we cannot see it. If you add a photo, you choose it with the iOS photo picker; Rank'd only receives the photo you select.

2. Apple Health

If you choose Import from Health and grant permission, Rank'd reads the following from Apple Health: VO₂ max, resting heart rate, step count, sleep analysis, workouts, body fat percentage, body weight and biological sex. Rank'd only reads this data; it never writes to Health. Health data is used on your device only to fill in your stats and calculate your scores. It is never sent to us or to any third party, and it is never used for advertising, marketing or data mining. You can change or revoke this permission at any time in the iOS Settings or Health app.

3. Friends you add

The Squad feature lets you add friends by entering their name, optional photo and stats yourself. This information is stored only on your device and is used only to show rankings and comparisons. Please add someone only with their permission, and only with information they are comfortable sharing with you.

4. Information we do not collect

Rank'd does not require an account and does not collect your contact details, location, contacts or device identifiers. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and we do not sell or share personal information.

5. How your information is used

The information you enter or import is used on your device only to calculate your scores, tier and level, display your cards, compare you with friends you've added, track your goals and progress, and show your history.

6. Sharing cards

If you share a card or a head-to-head comparison, the app creates an image and opens the iOS share sheet. The image is sent only to the app or person you choose, and that app's privacy policy applies from then on. Before sharing, you can choose in Settings whether shared cards show category scores, money or income, wellness details and other sensitive stats.

7. Game Center leaderboard (optional)

The leaderboard is off by default. If you turn on Publish my OVR, Rank'd signs in to Apple's Game Center and submits your overall score only — no category scores, stats or profile details. Your score appears on the leaderboard with your Game Center name and is handled by Apple under its privacy policy. You can turn the leaderboard off at any time in Settings.

8. App lock, backups and diagnostics

If you turn on the app lock, iOS checks your Face ID, Touch ID or passcode; Rank'd never sees your biometric data. Because your data is stored on your device, it may be included in your device backups (such as iCloud or computer backups) according to your own settings; those backups are handled by Apple under its privacy policy. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iOS settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports.

9. Deleting your data

You can edit or remove any stat in the app, remove friends from your squad, use Reset All Data in Settings to remove your profile, stats, goals, friends and progress history, or delete the app to remove all data it has stored on your device. Deleting data in Rank'd does not delete data stored in Apple Health.

10. Contacting us

If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your name, email address and message. We use it only to respond to you and to improve the app, and we delete it when it is no longer needed. Please do not send us your personal stats, health information or financial details.

11. Children

Rank'd is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through support, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date above will show when it was last updated, and material changes will be communicated when required.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the contact form or email [SUPPORT EMAIL].
