Privacy

Your photos stay on your phone.

Board recognition runs entirely on your iPhone. ChessCapture doesn't upload your photos anywhere, and the copies it keeps are deleted automatically within 24 hours.

  • On-device recognitionNo servers involved in reading the board.
  • Auto-deleted after 24 hoursScanned photos don't pile up.
