Common questions

TrackLore found the song but not the show. Why?

Soundtrack coverage varies by song, and not every identified song has a matching soundtrack entry yet. If you know where it's from, report it from the result.

Why does TrackLore need the microphone?

To recognize the music playing around you. Recognition also needs an internet connection.

How do I remove ads?

Buy Remove Ads Forever in the app. It's one payment — no subscription.

Will my library sync to my other devices?

Yes — privately through your iCloud account, on supported devices. You can also export your library as JSON or CSV.

Privacy policy

TrackLore · Effective October 18, 2026

TrackLore helps you identify music and learn where songs appear in movies, television, anime, games, and other media. This policy explains what information TrackLore processes, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information TrackLore processes

Recognition and catalog queries

TrackLore uses Apple's ShazamKit framework to recognize music. TrackLore does not upload or store the microphone recording on its own backend. After a match, the app sends song metadata such as title, artist, and, when available, ISRC to TrackLore's backend so it can return media, episode, scene, soundtrack, related song, and source information. Manual search terms are also sent to the backend.

These queries are used to provide results, prevent abuse, maintain service availability, and diagnose failures. TrackLore does not use a query to build an advertising profile. Hosting and security systems may process network metadata such as IP address for delivery, security, and short-lived operational logs.

Support and catalog corrections

The support form asks for a name, email address, subject, and message. A catalog correction contains the affected song and source, the selected issue type, free-form details, and an optional reply email. A correction may be submitted without an email address. TrackLore does not intentionally store a raw IP address or device identifier with a correction report.

To prevent abuse, security counters use a keyed one-way network-address hash. Per-address counters expire within two minutes of the last request; they are not linked to report contents or used for advertising. Daily aggregate intake counters contain no network address and may remain for up to two days. Hosting provider logs and backups are kept for limited periods under those providers' own retention practices.

Correction reports and optional reply emails are automatically removed from the TrackLore database after 365 days. Support messages are delivered to TrackLore's support mailbox through Brevo and are deleted when no longer needed to respond, protect the service, or meet legal obligations. You may request earlier deletion at support@tracklore.app.

Private iCloud library and notes

You may sync saved matches, favorites, collections, private notes, challenge results, and listening statistics through Apple's CloudKit. This data is stored in your private iCloud database and is used only to synchronize TrackLore on devices signed in to that Apple Account. TrackLore does not use it for advertising. You can remove entries in TrackLore and can manage or delete the app's iCloud data through your Apple Account and iCloud settings.

Purchases and app integrity

Purchases are made through Apple's StoreKit. TrackLore does not receive payment card details. The optional lifetime purchase removes advertising; all app features are otherwise available without purchase. Existing active subscriptions and earlier lifetime purchases continue to remove ads.

For existing Premium subscribers using reserved backend capacity, the app sends an Apple-signed subscription transaction and an App Attest proof to TrackLore's backend. The backend verifies the proof, discards the raw transaction after the request, and retains an Apple-attested per-install public key, anti-replay counter, last-use time, and keyed one-way entitlement binding. This information is used only for entitlement verification, fraud prevention, and service security.

Advertising and consent

The free version uses Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform. Depending on region, consent, device settings, and ad configuration, Google may process device identifiers, coarse location inferred from IP address, advertising data, product interactions, crash data, performance data, and other diagnostic data for advertising and analytics. Where required, TrackLore asks for consent and App Tracking Transparency permission. A verified lifetime ad-removal purchase, or an existing active Plus or Premium subscription, removes ads from the app, but previously collected information remains subject to the applicable provider's retention rules.

Links to other services

Results may include links to Apple Music, iTunes, Spotify, and other sources. When you open one of those links, that service's own privacy policy applies.

2. How information is used

TrackLore uses information to:

  • provide recognition, search, catalog details, sync, support, and corrections;
  • verify purchases and protect Premium reserved capacity;
  • prevent abuse, investigate errors, and keep the service reliable;
  • display and measure advertising in the free version, subject to available consent and device controls; and
  • comply with law, enforce terms, and protect users and the service.

TrackLore does not sell personal information and does not use private iCloud library content or notes for advertising.

3. Service providers

Information is processed only as needed by providers supporting these features:

  • Apple: ShazamKit, StoreKit, App Attest, CloudKit, and App Store services;
  • Google: Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform;
  • Render: backend hosting;
  • Neon: database hosting; and
  • Brevo and TrackLore's email provider: delivery and handling of support and correction messages.

Each provider processes information under its own terms and privacy policy. TrackLore may also disclose information when required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety, users, or the service.

4. Retention and security

TrackLore limits collection to the data needed for each feature, uses encrypted HTTPS connections, stores server secrets outside the app in the hosting provider's encrypted secret settings, and uses Apple-signed StoreKit proof plus App Attest for protected Premium routing. No security method is perfect, but TrackLore reviews access and limits retained information by purpose.

Correction reports are retained for no more than 365 days. Private CloudKit data remains until you remove it through the app or iCloud. App Attest security records are retained while needed to protect active Premium access and prevent replay or fraud. Other operational and provider records are kept only as long as needed for their stated purposes, legal obligations, dispute resolution, and security.

5. Your choices

You can:

  • deny microphone access, although recognition will not work;
  • manage advertising consent in the app when the Google privacy-options entry is available, and manage tracking permission in iOS Settings;
  • buy lifetime ad removal or restore an existing ad-free purchase;
  • omit an email address from a correction report;
  • remove saved items and notes in TrackLore and manage TrackLore's private data in iCloud settings; and
  • contact support@tracklore.app to request access, correction, or deletion of support or correction information associated with your email.

The ability to exercise a legal privacy right may depend on your location. We may need to verify a request before acting on it.

6. Children

TrackLore is not directed to children under the minimum age required to consent to data processing in their country. Do not submit contact information or free-form notes for a child without appropriate authorization.

7. International processing and changes

Providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, TrackLore and its providers use appropriate safeguards. TrackLore may update this policy as features, providers, or laws change. The published policy will show its effective date, and material changes will be communicated when required.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact support@tracklore.app.
